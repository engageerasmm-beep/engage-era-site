What Is AEO? How Brands Get Recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity

Search is moving from ten blue links to one answer. Here's what answer engine optimization is, and what brands can actually control.

Ben Meller·Sep 29, 2026·6 min read
The short versionAEO (answer engine optimization) means making your brand easy for AI tools to find, understand and recommend. You can't buy your way into an AI answer, but you can make your brand clear, consistent and cited across the web.

For twenty years, getting found online meant ranking on page one of Google. That still matters. But a growing share of people now ask an AI tool the question directly, get one answer back, and never click a link at all.

If your brand isn't part of that answer, you don't exist in that moment. AEO, or answer engine optimization, is the work of changing that.

What AEO actually means

An "answer engine" is any tool that responds to a question with a written answer instead of a list of links: ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot. AEO is making your brand easy for those tools to find, understand, trust and recommend.

Think of it as the next layer on top of SEO. SEO gets your page ranked. AEO gets your brand named.

How AI tools decide what to say

Each tool works differently and none publish their full recipe, but the broad pattern is public. Many answer engines search the web in real time and cite the pages they used. Perplexity shows its sources on every answer. Google's own guidance says there are no special optimizations needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and that the same SEO fundamentals apply: crawlable pages, quality content and a good page experience.

In practice, AI answers lean on what the web already says about you, and how consistently it says it.

Six things brands can control

1. Say plainly who you are

Put one clear sentence on your site that describes your business the way you'd want an AI to repeat it: what you do, who you do it for, and where. Vague taglines are great for billboards and useless to a language model.

2. Be the same brand everywhere

Your name, description, location and links should match across your website, Google Business Profile, Instagram, LinkedIn and any directories. Inconsistent details make it harder for any system to be sure they're all the same business.

3. Answer the questions people actually ask

Write pages and FAQ sections that answer real customer questions in plain language, with the answer near the top. "How much does a social media agency cost?" is a better heading than "Our Philosophy."

4. Add structured data

Structured data (schema markup) is code that labels your content for machines: this is an organization, this is its address, these are its FAQs, this article was written by this person. Google documents the supported types, and it's one of the cleanest ways to remove ambiguity.

5. Earn mentions on sites that are already trusted

Press features, podcast appearances, partner pages, reviews and industry roundups all add to what the web says about you. AI tools that search the web can only cite what exists.

6. Keep it fresh

Outdated pages, old prices and dead links send mixed signals. A site that publishes regularly and keeps its core pages current is easier to trust, for people and for machines.

What you can't control

You can't pay for placement inside an organic AI answer, and you can't guarantee a mention. Answers also vary by tool, by wording and by user. Treat AEO like reputation: you build the conditions, then measure over time.

How to check where you stand

Open ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity and ask the questions your customers ask: "best social media agency in Palm Beach," "who can help me grow my brand on Instagram," or whatever fits your business. Note who gets named and which sources get cited. Repeat monthly. That's your baseline.

The takeaway

AEO isn't a trick. It's clarity, consistency and proof, published where machines can read it. The brands that win in AI answers will be the ones that were easiest to understand.

FAQ

What is AEO?

AEO, or answer engine optimization, is the practice of making a brand easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity to find, understand and recommend in their answers.

Is AEO different from SEO?

They overlap. SEO focuses on ranking pages in search results. AEO focuses on being the source or brand an AI answer names. Strong SEO fundamentals still help, because many AI tools draw on web search results.

Can you pay to appear in AI answers?

Not in the organic answer itself. What you can do is make your brand clear and consistent online, publish content that answers real questions, and earn mentions on sites AI tools already trust.
