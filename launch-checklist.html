Edit the existing records with those names. If there's more than one A record for @, or any AAAA record for @, delete the extras. If GoDaddy says the domain is connected to Website Builder, disconnect that first.

Do not touch: MX, TXT, SRV, or CNAMEs named autodiscover, lyncdiscover, sip, enterpriseregistration, enterpriseenrollment. Those run your Microsoft email.
